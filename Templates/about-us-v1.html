A Message From Our Founder

Welcome to RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD

It is my pleasure to welcome you to an institution founded with a clear vision—to equip individuals, businesses, and communities with the practical digital and technology skills required to thrive in an increasingly technology-driven world.

Technology is transforming every industry. Artificial intelligence, software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud computing, automation, robotics, and data science are reshaping how people live, learn, and work. At RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD, we believe that access to quality technology education should empower people to participate confidently in this transformation.

Our institution was established to bridge the gap between traditional education and the rapidly evolving demands of the global digital economy. We recognize that employers increasingly seek professionals who possess practical skills, critical thinking abilities, adaptability, and a commitment to lifelong learning. For this reason, our programmes emphasize experiential learning, project-based instruction, industry-relevant tools, and real-world applications.

Our commitment extends beyond technical competence. We strive to cultivate innovation, creativity, ethical leadership, collaboration, and professionalism in every learner. Whether training a child taking their first steps into coding, a university graduate seeking career-ready skills, a working professional advancing their expertise, or an organization investing in workforce development, our goal is to provide learning experiences that create lasting impact.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, management, faculty, and staff, I warmly welcome you to RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD. Together, we will ignite potential, inspire innovation, and empower the future.

Bilkis Shehu
Founder & Chief Executive Officer, RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD
About Us

Who We Are

RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD is a private technology education and professional training institution established to provide high-quality, practical, and industry-relevant education in emerging technologies, digital transformation, and professional development. Headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, the institution is dedicated to preparing learners with the knowledge, technical competencies, and professional skills required to succeed in a rapidly evolving global digital economy.

Recognizing that technological advancement is redefining the future of education, employment, entrepreneurship, and business operations, RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD has adopted an educational model that integrates academic excellence with practical application. Through instructor-led training, hands-on laboratories, collaborative projects, case studies, simulations, and industry engagement, learners acquire competencies that are immediately applicable in professional environments.

The institution serves a diverse learner population, including children, secondary school students, university undergraduates, graduates, professionals, entrepreneurs, public sector personnel, corporate organizations, and community groups. Programmes are designed to accommodate learners at varying stages of their educational and professional journeys, providing clear pathways for foundational learning, specialization, and continuous professional development.

RUBYTECH LEARNING LTD is guided by principles of innovation, integrity, inclusiveness, excellence, and lifelong learning. By fostering an environment that encourages curiosity, creativity, critical thinking, and collaboration, the institution aims to produce graduates who are capable of solving real-world challenges through technology and innovation, contributing meaningfully to national development and the advancement of digital skills across Africa.
